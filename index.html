Word Family

French traditionally sorts its verbs into 3 groups — but the logic isn't quite the same as in Italian.

1st group — verbs in -er (except aller): about 90% of French verbs, all conjugated on the same regular model.

2nd group — verbs in -ir with the -iss- infix (finir → nous finissons, ils finissent): it's this group that matches the logic of -isc- in Italian (capire → capisco), not the 3rd.

3rd group — everything else: a genuine catch-all of irregular verbs, with several internal families that don't necessarily resemble each other (“short” -ir verbs like partir, the venir/tenir family, -re verbs, -oir verbs, and a handful of totally irregular verbs like être and avoir).
